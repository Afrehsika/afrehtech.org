Smarter School Administration Starts Here.

AssessVault is an educational technology platform designed to help schools organize student information, manage assessments, generate report cards, and monitor academic performance more efficiently.

Visit AssessVault Schedule a School Demo

Key Problems AssessVault Solves for Schools

End of Term Delays

Eliminates weeks of manual paper grading, math errors, and stressful terminal collation for headteachers and teachers.

Lost or Altered Records

Replaces fragile paper ledgers with tamper-evident digital records safely stored and instantly searchable across academic years.

Parent Communication Gap

Empowers parents with timely terminal reports, attendance clarity, and performance breakdowns on their children's progress.

Core Platform Modules

Student Records Management

Centralized student bio data, class allocations, admission numbers, and complete attendance tracking.

SBA Gradebook Management

Tailored for Ghanaian curriculum standards: continuous assessment, class tests, homework, and exam marks.

Automated Report Cards

Instant PDF report card synthesis with school crest, calculated positions, class teacher remarks, and grade keys.

Academic Performance Analytics

Visual bar and line charts diagnosing student trajectories, class averages, and subject pass percentages.

Mock Examination Analysis

Rigorous mock exam tracking for final year students preparing for BECE and WASSCE examinations.

Target Institutions

Designed specifically for Private and Public Basic Schools, Junior High, and Senior High Schools in Ghana.
